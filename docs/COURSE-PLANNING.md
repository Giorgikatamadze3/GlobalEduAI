# Course modules — planning only

Sprint 1 scope: define requirements, not publish courses or accept payments.

## Crash Course

Audience: students preparing an international master's application. Suggested units: selecting a verified programme, interpreting entry requirements, GPA and transcript review, language certificates, funding eligibility, CV/motivation letter, application deadlines and a final submission checklist.

MVP: course overview with prerequisites and learning outcomes; ordered lessons; accessible text/video with transcripts; short knowledge checks; account-based progress; downloadable checklist. Completion signifies finishing the material, not university admission or accredited certification.

Acceptance: a student can start, resume and complete a lesson on another device; unsupported video has a text alternative; course edits retain progress or explicitly version the course; advisors cannot edit content without administrator permission.

## Business Management Course

Audience: prospective students exploring business studies. Suggested units: management fundamentals, strategy, marketing, accounting and finance basics, operations, people management, business analytics, ethics and a final case study.

MVP: introductory course with learning outcomes, lesson sequence, practical examples, quizzes and a case-study worksheet. No claim of university credit, formal qualification or professional accreditation.

Acceptance: each lesson has an outcome and exercise; quiz feedback explains the answer; course completion requires all mandatory lessons and the final assessment; course language and expected study effort are stated.
